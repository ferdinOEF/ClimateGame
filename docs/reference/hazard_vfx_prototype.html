Cyclone and flood: how they could look in the game

A live mock-up on the same hex tiles as Panaji. Pick a hazard, press play or drag the timeline, and switch defences on and off to see how the water reaches fewer homes.

What you are seeing

Cyclone

  • A rotating cloud spiral with an eye drifts in from the sea and darkens the sky.
  • Wave bands roll in, foam-edged, and grow as the storm nears.
  • Storm surge lifts the water level; tiles flood from the shore inland and drain afterwards.
  • Palms bend in the wind, rain streaks slant, lightning flickers at landfall.

Flood

  • Rain builds first; then the river tiles physically rise as a swell travels downriver toward the sea, deepening from blue to a dark, churning blue.
  • A pale crest moves along the river and dark blue water spills over low, unprotected banks, choppy with white flecks and flow streaks.
  • Khazans fill with teal stored water and protect homes beside them.

Cyclone + flood (finale)

  • The storm and the rain arrive together. The sea surge pushes up against the swollen river.
  • Backwater: near the mouth the river cannot drain, so it rises higher and floods the low land from both sides.
  • Light turquoise sea water and dark navy river water overlap in an indigo mix, showing where both fronts hit the same homes.

Defences

  • Mangrove belt: waves lose strength behind it and a ring pulses where they hit.
  • Rows behind the belt stay dry; rows outside it flood. This is the "where to focus" view.
