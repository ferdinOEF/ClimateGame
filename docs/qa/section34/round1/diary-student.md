# Playtest diary: student (13 to 15, Goa)

## 1. Who I am and what I wanted

I'm a school student from Goa. I know Miramar, Campal and Merces, so the map of Panjim made me curious straight away. I want to get going fast, I don't read long boxes, and I want to win. I skipped the Tutorial and clicked "Play Panjim" right away. My goal was to get to the end and get all the stars.

## 2. The play, in order

- **Menu (about 5 s).** I skipped the Tutorial and pressed Play Panjim. The intro box had a lot of text. I read only the bold line ("Save as many homes as you can from three storms") and looked at the red map with the arrow pointing at Sharada Mandir School.
- **Glitch at the start.** My first press on "Begin" didn't work. The game was back on the main menu and the Begin button was gone. I pressed Play Panjim again and then Begin worked. Not sure why.
- **2026 to 2030, cyclone build-up (5 builds).** Maya told me to look at the card at the bottom right, which said "1 dune and 2 pandanus in front of the school". The tiles to use were outlined in yellow, so that was easy. My first build was a dune, at about **33 seconds and 7 actions**. After the first pandanus, Homes at risk went 9 -> 6, and after the second it went to 0. That felt good. Next came "Get ahead: build a khazan in Merces". I zoomed out, found Merces and built it. For my last build before the storm I clicked a tile in the sea by mistake and got Seawall or Breakwater. I picked Breakwater because it sounded cool.
- **Cyclone 2030.** It came straight after my 5th build. There was rain and water over the beach tiles. It was over in about 10 seconds, so I didn't even need Speed up. **3 stars, 9 of 9 homes saved.** "What protected your homes" said Pandanus 9, and Breakwater and Dune "Shared the work", but those words were cut off.
- **2031 to 2040, flood build-up (10 builds).** The card said: 1 mangrove in Taleigao, 3 khazans in St Cruz, 2 mangroves in Merces, 2 khazans in Merces. Homes at risk was 50. Several yellow tiles were outlined, but nothing told me which tile belonged to which place or job. I just clicked them and watched which counter went up. One khazan I built on a yellow tile didn't count for St Cruz, even though Homes at risk dropped a lot (29 -> 20). I think that tile was really the Taleigao mangrove spot, so I used it for the wrong thing. After that there was no yellow tile left for Taleigao. I hunted around and clicked water tiles. One offered only a "Small dam" ("This doesn't change the next storm"). In the end I put two mangroves near the red houses, but neither ticked the Taleigao box. Homes at risk ended at 14.
- **Flood 2040.** The river turned dark blue and the houses went red. It looked dramatic and cool. **2 stars, 48 of 57 saved.** That's 84% and three stars needs 85%, so I missed by one home. The game only told me the % rules on the very last card.
- **2041 to 2050, cyclone + flood build-up (10 builds).** The card said "Plant 3 mangroves at 3 places, shown on the map" and "Build 5 khazans at 3 places, shown on the map". Again the places don't say which build they want. I got mangroves to 2/3 and khazans to 4/5. Two of my builds on outlined tiles counted for nothing on the card. Maya said "Campal is most at risk". With 2 builds left I put a **Seawall** ("the strongest wall") on the beach in front of Campal. **Homes at risk stayed at 13.** It cost 5 biodiversity and did nothing. Then I planted a pandanus, and the storm started right away.
- **Cyclone and flood 2050.** Campal's houses went red. I skipped the replay. **2 stars, 41 of 54 saved.** The breakdown showed Seawall 0, Pandanus 3, Mangrove 25, Khazan 28.
- **Final card.** 98 of 120 homes, +49 biodiversity, **7 of 9 stars, Panjim cleared.**

## 3. What confused me

1. **Which outlined tile is for which job.** The checklist names places (Taleigao, St Cruz, Merces) and the map outlines tiles, but you can't tell which outline goes with which line, or whether it wants a mangrove or a khazan. I wasted at least 3 builds on outlined tiles that didn't count. *Would help:* a small icon on each outlined tile showing what to build there, or when I hover a checklist line, light up only its tiles.
2. **I couldn't find the Taleigao mangrove spot.** After I built a khazan on what was probably that spot, no outline was left anywhere. *Would help:* if a slot gets used by the wrong build, say so ("This spot wanted a mangrove"), or show the next place to do it.
3. **The seawall did nothing.** It's described as "The strongest wall against cyclone surge", but Homes at risk didn't move and the breakdown said 0. Nothing explained why. *Would help:* show the change in Homes at risk on the build card before I build, the way "This doesn't change the next storm" already appears for some builds. Show it for every option.
4. **Two different home numbers.** Homes at risk said 50 at the start of the flood build-up, but the result said "of 57". At the start of the last build-up it said 29, but the result said "of 54". I didn't get why they're different.
5. **The star rules came too late.** I only learned 85% = 3 stars on the very last screen. I missed 3 stars in 2040 by one home (48/57 = 84%). *Would help:* show the star targets during the build-up, for example "3 stars: save 49".
6. **Clicking a house shows a "House" bubble**, and the next click only closes it. A few times I clicked a tile and nothing happened, because that click was spent closing the bubble. Maya's "Got it" button also moves up and down a bit between tips, so I sometimes missed it and hit the map.
7. **No way to see which tile I had selected.** When the build menu opened, nothing on the map showed which hex it was for.

## 4. Where I was bored

- The intro box and the Maya facts ("Studies found 100 m of mangroves cut wave height by 13% to 66%..."). I skipped them all.
- The middle of each 10-build stretch, where I was just clicking outlines and watching counters. The 10 builds before the flood felt long, because the board barely changes and the storm is far away.
- I didn't watch replays. I skipped the last one.

## 5. Where I smiled

- Homes at risk dropping 9 -> 6 -> 0 when I planted pandanus in front of the school.
- The birds (a white egret and a blue kingfisher) flying over the map.
- The flood turning the whole river dark blue with rain. That looked really good.
- Seeing real places I know (Miramar Beach, Sharada Mandir, Dhempe College, Kala Academy, Don Bosco).
- "Panjim cleared".

## 6. Bugs and glitches

1. **Begin didn't start the game; it went back to the main menu** (major, first impression). Steps: main menu -> Play Panjim -> intro box shows -> click Begin. Got: the main menu again, with no Begin button. Clicking Play Panjim again and then Begin worked. Expected: Begin starts the game first time. Screenshot: student-first-board.jpg (taken after the second, working try). This happened once and I couldn't make it happen again.
2. **"Shared the work" text cut off** in "What protected your homes" (minor). Steps: finish the 2030 cyclone -> See what protected your city. Got: "Shared the" clipped at the right edge, next to full grey bars. Expected: the whole label shows. Screenshot: student-protected-clipped.jpg.
3. **Outlined target tiles don't count for the checklist** (major for winning). Steps: in the 2031 build-up, build a khazan on the outlined tile at the west end of the St Cruz group. Got: St Cruz stayed at 2/3 and Taleigao stayed at 0/1, but Homes at risk dropped. The same thing happened twice in the 2041 build-up (one khazan, one mangrove on outlined tiles, neither counted). Expected: each outline says what it is for, or any matching build on it counts. Screenshot: student-no-mangrove-spot.jpg (no outline left for the Taleigao mangrove).
4. **Seawall changed nothing** (major for trust, maybe intended). Steps: 2049, beach in front of Campal -> Seawall. Got: Homes at risk stayed at 13, biodiversity went down 5, and the final breakdown said Seawall 0. Expected: the "strongest wall" saves something, or the game warns me first. Screenshot: student-seawall-no-change.jpg.
5. **Biodiversity number lags** (minor). Right after the Breakwater (-2) the top bar still said +10, and it only changed to +8 on the result card. In the last stretch the bar said +46 before the storm and +49 after. Expected: it updates as soon as I build.
6. **Clicks swallowed by the "House" bubble** (minor). Steps: click a house tile, and a "House" bubble appears. Click an outlined tile. Got: nothing, and a second click is needed. Expected: one click opens the build menu.
7. **Labels jump around and pile up** (minor). In the last stretch, the building labels (Institute Menezes Braganza, Adil Shah Palace, Kala Academy, Government Polytechnic...) moved position between builds and covered the map near Campal. Screenshot: student-label-clutter.jpg.
8. **Console:** the browser console showed nothing at the end (no errors or warnings).

## 7. Fun signals

- **First build:** about 33 seconds, 7 actions (including the Begin glitch).
- **Whole run:** about 8 minutes of real-player time.
- **Real choices** (moments with at least two good options that lead to different outcomes): about **4**.
  1. 2029, Merces tile: Mangrove ("both storms") or Khazan (what the checklist asked for). I picked Khazan.
  2. 2030, sea tile: Seawall or Breakwater. I picked Breakwater.
  3. 2040, last build before the flood, near Taleigao's red houses: Mangrove or Khazan. I picked Mangrove.
  4. 2049, Campal beach: Seawall, Pandanus or Dune. I picked Seawall, and it was the wrong call.
  Most of the other 21 builds weren't real choices. I just followed the checklist outlines.
- **Did one defence always win?** Yes: khazans and mangroves on the outlined river tiles. They gave 29 + 11 in 2040 and 28 + 25 in 2050. Pandanus won the beach. Seawall was useless (0). If you follow the checklist you basically win.
- **Storm results that surprised me:**
  - The 2030 cyclone was over in about 10 seconds.
  - The seawall saved 0 homes.
  - 48/57 was only 2 stars.
  - In 2040 houses got hit in Merces even after I did everything the Merces checklist asked.
- **Why I lost homes, after each storm:**
  - *2030 cyclone:* I lost none. The pandanus in front of the school did all the work, and the game made that clear (Pandanus 9).
  - *2040 flood:* I lost 9, I think because I never did the Taleigao mangrove and some houses near Fontainhas and Merces were outside what I protected. The game showed red houses in the replay, but it didn't say *which missing build* would have saved them, so only half clear.
  - *2050 cyclone and flood:* I lost 13, mostly the houses around Campal by the beach. My seawall there did nothing and my pandanus only saved 3. Maya had warned "Campal is most at risk" but not what actually works there, so not very clear.

## 9. Would I play again? Would I tell a friend?

- **Play again:** yes, once. I want 9 stars and I was one home away in 2040. But the second time I'd just be copying the checklist outlines, so I'm not sure about a third time.
- **Tell a friend:** maybe. I'd tell them: "It's Panjim with storms, it looks cool and you get to save Miramar and Campal. Just do what the card in the corner says, put pandanus on the beach and khazans by the river, and don't build the seawall, it does nothing."

## 10. Verdict

Looks great and starts fast, but after the first storm it's "click the yellow outline" without telling you what goes where, and the one bold choice I made (the seawall) did nothing and nobody told me why.

## Knowledge questions (asked after play)

1. **What does a mangrove do in a storm?** It protects against waves and surge in both kinds of storm. Maya said mangroves slow the surge before it reaches the houses, and that 100 m of mangroves can cut wave height by about 13% to 66%.
2. **Why can a seawall be risky?** The card said it "costs nature, and cracks with age", and it took 5 off my biodiversity. In my game it also saved 0 homes, but the game never told me why.
3. **What is a khazan, and what is it for?** Maya said khazans are Goa's coastal wetlands, reclaimed from marsh, with bunds that keep out the tide, and farmers and fishers share the land. It holds back river floods: it holds the floodwater in its fields, so nearby homes stay drier.
4. **Where did the 2040 flood come from, and why from there rather than the sea?** Maya said it comes down the Mandovi, through the channel at Ribandar, into Merces, St Cruz and Taleigao. Why it comes from the river and not the sea: I don't know. The game only called it a "monsoon flood" and never explained.
5. **What helps homes against a cyclone's wind?** Pandanus: its card said it "shields homes from cyclone waves and wind". That's the only build I remember mentioning wind.
