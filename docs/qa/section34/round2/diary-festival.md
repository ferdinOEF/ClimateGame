# Diary: festival visitor, round 2

## 1. Who I am and what I wanted

I'm a visitor at a festival in Panjim. I walked up to a big screen with a hex map of my own city on it and had about 10 minutes. I skim, I click whatever looks inviting, and I never opened "?" or "Facts & sources". I wanted to see if I could save Panjim, and to recognise places I know (Miramar, Campal, Merces, Taleigao).

I skipped the Tutorial and went straight to **Play Panjim**. (I did end up in the Tutorial once by accident; see bugs.)

## 2. The play, in order

I had to start Play Panjim three times. The first two runs were thrown back to the main menu partway through (see bug 1). Below: run 1 to the point it died, then run 3, the full run. Run 2 was the same as run 3 up to 2039 and died there.

**Run 1, 2026 to 2031 (lost).**
- Intro card: I read the bold line "Save as many homes as you can from three storms" and the map title "The first cyclone lands in 5 years", then pressed Begin. Skipped the paragraph.
- Maya and a checklist (bottom right): "Build 1 dune / Plant 2 pandanus in front of Sharada Mandir School". Three beach hexes were outlined. I clicked one and the menu had Dune marked **Objective**, so I built it. Homes at risk went from 9 to 4 right away. Good feeling.
- Pandanus twice. Homes at risk 0. A new list item said "Build 1 khazan in Merces". I didn't know where Merces was, so I clicked the list item and the camera **flew me there** with the hex outlined. Very nice. Built the khazan (Mangrove was also offered).
- Last year before the cyclone: I clicked the sea and built a **Breakwater** because it sounded cool.
- **Cyclone 2030:** a bit of rain and a faint swirl, then it was over in a few seconds. 3 stars, 9 of 9 saved, Miramar Beach "Stood".
- The flood preview said 10 years away. I clicked on to 2031 and got a list of 4 items (mangrove in Taleigao, 3 khazans in St Cruz, and so on), homes at risk 50. I clicked the Taleigao item, looked around, clicked a tile, and was suddenly in the **Tutorial** at "Year 1 of 7". Then the main menu. Run gone.

**Run 3, the full run (run 2 went the same way up to 2039).**
- **2026 to 2030:** dune, pandanus, pandanus, khazan in Merces. For the last build I tried a mangrove beside the khazan instead of a breakwater.
- **Cyclone 2030:** 3 stars, 9 of 9 saved. This time **Miramar Beach "Damaged"**. With the breakwater in run 1 it had "Stood". That is a real visible difference, and I liked spotting it.
- "What protected your homes": Dune 5, Pandanus 4. Short and readable.
- **2031 to 2040 (10 builds for the flood):** I clicked whatever hex was outlined and took the **Objective** option (mangroves, khazans). Homes at risk dropped 49 → 44 → 36 → 29 → 14. A heron or kingfisher flew across the map. Then "Objective done", plus one more item, "Plant 1 mangrove at Taleigao". I did it, but homes at risk stayed at **14**.
- Maya: "Last call! 14 homes are still in danger, most of them around Taleigao." Red-striped house tiles showed which ones. I clicked one ("House", nothing to build), then a blank land tile (nothing), then a channel tile, which offered a **Small dam**. Built it hoping it would help.
- **Flood 2040:** blue water everywhere, then a replay: "14 homes hit, 43 homes wet but standing". Exactly the 14 it had warned me about. **2 stars**, 43 of 57 saved. The card told me "three stars allow 8 at most".
- **2041 to 2050 (10 builds for cyclone and flood together):** the screen was crowded with landmark labels (Adil Shah Palace, Kala Academy, Don Bosco...). The list said "Build 6 khazans at 5 places, shown on the map". I built them on the outlined hexes. Then Maya: "The list is done. Press R to see the homes this storm would still take". I pressed R: red houses all along the **Campal shore**, 12 at risk, 4 builds left.
- I built a **seawall** on the Campal beach ("the strongest wall"). Still 12. A **breakwater** in front: still 12. I started reading the build menu more closely and saw that some tiles say "**This doesn't change the next storm**". I hunted for a tile without that note, built a second **seawall** in the sea there (12 → 11), and put my last build, a **pandanus**, on a beach tile that also lacked the note.
- **Cyclone and flood 2050:** 9 homes lost. **2 stars.** The card said "The wind alone took 8 of the homes lost. In this game pandanus shields the homes within 2 tiles of it." It also said biodiversity was +37, and three stars need +40. My two seawalls (−5 each) cost me that star.
- Final card: **96 of 119 homes saved, 7 of 9 stars, "Panjim cleared".** Play again or Back to the menu.

## 3. What confused me

1. **Being thrown back to the menu (twice), and once into the Tutorial.** I had no idea what happened. *Would help:* a big "Still there?" countdown I can't miss, and a "Continue your Panjim run" button on the menu.
2. **"Homes at risk" would not go down, and I couldn't tell why.** In 2040 (14) and in 2048 (12) I built things and the number didn't move. *Would help:* show the "This doesn't change the next storm" note as a big red tag on the tile *before* I open the menu. Or make the outlined "suggested" hexes keep appearing after the checklist is done.
3. **The storm 3 checklist was only khazans, but the homes I lost were on the Campal beach, and to wind.** Maya even said "The beach and the wetlands both need you", but the list never gave me a beach item. *Would help:* one beach item in that list, e.g. "Plant pandanus at Campal".
4. **Wind as a separate threat** only showed up in the last result card. I'd built seawalls thinking "strongest = best". *Would help:* the tile menu could say "wind" vs "surge" more loudly, or a Maya line before storm 3 such as "this one brings strong wind".
5. **Biodiversity stars.** I didn't know seawalls would cost me a star until the very end. *Would help:* show "−5 biodiversity: you need +40 by 2050" when I hover Seawall late in the game.
6. "**Build 6 khazans at 5 places**": 6 at 5? I just clicked outlined tiles and it worked out, but the wording made me stop.
7. A mangrove I planted in Merces before storm 1 didn't count toward "Plant 2 mangroves in Merces" (0/2) later. *Would help:* count it, or don't show the hex as Merces.
8. Pressing **R** is a keyboard thing. At a festival big screen there may be no keyboard in reach. *Would help:* an on-screen button "Show homes still at risk".

## 4. Where I was bored

- The **10 builds before the flood** and the **10 before storm 3**. Once I'd learned "click the outlined hex, pick Objective", it was a click-click loop with no tension for a long stretch.
- **Cyclone 1** itself: rain, a faint swirl, over. I didn't see a wave hit anything. It's the first storm and it felt like nothing happened.
- The pile of result cards after each storm (result, then "what protected", then "coming up"). Three clicks of reading each time. I skimmed them all.

## 5. Where I smiled

- Homes at risk dropping from 9 to 4 to 0 the moment I built: instant feedback.
- Clicking a checklist item and the camera **flying** to Merces with the tile outlined.
- Seeing real neighbourhoods and landmarks of Panjim on the map.
- The bird flying over the map.
- Run 1 vs run 3: breakwater gave "Miramar Beach Stood", without it "Damaged". I noticed it myself and felt clever.
- The flood replay with "dark: lost, pale: wet but standing". Easy to read.
- "Panjim cleared" at the end.

## 9. Would I play again? Would I tell a friend?

- **Play again: yes, but only at home**, not at the festival. I want to beat the third star on storm 3 with pandanus instead of seawalls. At the festival, no: losing two runs to the menu would have made me leave.
- **Tell a friend: yes.** I'd say: "It's Panjim as a hex board. You get one build a year and storms come. Follow Maya's list, use pandanus on the beach, don't spam seawalls. And don't walk away from it for a minute or it resets."

## 10. Verdict

A charming, readable Panjim coast game whose follow-the-list loop works. But it silently threw away my run twice, and it only told me about wind and biodiversity after the storm that punished me.

## Knowledge questions (asked after play)

1. **What does a mangrove do in a storm?** It slows the water before it reaches the houses, in a cyclone's surge and waves and in river floods too (the build menu, and Maya: "plant them on the wetland edge"). After the flood, Maya added that mangroves slowed the water through their roots and sheltered the streets behind them.
2. **Why can a seawall be risky?** It costs nature (−5 biodiversity each, which cost me my third star) and it "cracks with age". Maya said it holds back surge only until it breaks: in its second cyclone, or in its next one once it is 10 years old.
3. **What is a khazan, and what is it for?** From the game: something that holds back river floods, that farmers and fishers share, and whose fields hold floodwater. Maya also said its bunds and sluice gates breach if they aren't kept up. Beyond "fields with bunds and sluice gates", I don't know exactly what it is.
4. **Where did the 2040 flood come from, and why from there rather than the sea?** It came down the Mandovi river, through the channel at Ribandar, into Merces, St Cruz and Taleigao. It came from there because it was monsoon rain swelling the river upstream, not a sea storm.
5. **What helps homes against a cyclone's wind?** Pandanus: the build menu says it shields homes from waves and wind, and the last result card said pandanus shields homes within 2 tiles of it. The game never named anything else for wind.
