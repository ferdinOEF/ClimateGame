# Playtest diary: student (13-15, Goa)

## 1. Who I am and what I wanted

I'm a school kid from Goa. I know Miramar, Campal, Panjim market. I want to start fast, I don't read long boxes, and I want to win. I skipped the Tutorial and hit "Play Panjim" straight away. My goal was to get to the last card with as many stars as possible.

## 2. The play, in order

Times are what a real player would spend, not my thinking time.

**Run 1 (lost, see bugs)**

- **0-12s, intro.** I skimmed the intro card. What I took from it: "save homes from three storms, first cyclone in 5 years". The red map looked cool. I clicked Begin.
- **2026-2028, the school.** Three hexes glow on Miramar beach, and the card bottom right says 1 dune plus 2 pandanus. I clicked a glowing hex and a menu popped up with Dune, Pandanus and Seawall. One option has an "Objective" tag, so I clicked that. **First build at about 28s (4 actions).** "Homes at risk" dropped 9 -> 8 -> 3 -> 0 with each build. That felt great: you can see your build working.
- **2029, Merces khazan.** The new task said "khazan in Merces". I had no idea where Merces was, so I zoomed out, found it on the right, and built it.
- **2030, last build before the cyclone.** Homes at risk was already 0, but I wanted to be extra safe, so I put a **Seawall** (the "strongest wall") on the beach to the north.
- **Cyclone 2030.** I hit Speed up and it was over in about 5 seconds. **2 stars.** The card said nature paid for it: biodiversity was +5 and three stars need +6. Then "What protected your homes" showed **Seawall 0**. So my seawall did nothing, and it cost me the third star. That was clear, and annoying in a good way: I learned something.
- **2031-2032, flood prep.** Homes at risk jumped to 50. There were 4 tasks, about 8 builds' worth, with 10 builds to do them. I planted a mangrove in Merces. Then on the next mangrove click, **the game threw me back to the main menu.** There was no "Still there?" warning, and I'd been clicking all along. When I pressed Play Panjim, it started over at 2026, year 1. Everything was gone. Then **it happened again** about 30 seconds after I pressed Play Panjim, before I'd even clicked Begin. I nearly quit.

**Run 2 (the full run)**

- **2026-2030.** Same as before, but fast: pandanus, dune, pandanus, the Merces khazan, and then a **dune** at Dona Paula instead of the seawall. **Cyclone 2030: 3 stars**, 9/9 homes, biodiversity +12. I learned from run 1. That felt good.
- **2031-2038, flood prep.** I clicked the glowing hexes: 2 mangroves and 2 khazans in Merces, 3 khazans in St Cruz, 1 mangrove in Taleigao. Homes at risk went 50 -> 14. A bird flew over the map. The clicking got a bit samey: glowing hex, Objective option, repeat.
- **2039-2040.** Objective done. Maya said "Taleigao is most at risk: strengthen it now", but the new task said "mangrove near Atal Setu". I put a khazan in water next to red-striped homes (14 -> 10), then the Atal Setu mangrove. The camera jumped somewhere else on its own between those two builds.
- **Flood 2040. 3 stars**, 51/57 homes saved. I sped it up, so I never saw where the 6 lost homes were.
- **2041-2049, the final prep.** The tasks were "2 mangroves at Taleigao and the Ourem creek" and "7 khazans at 5 places, shown on the map". The map was covered in labels: Adil Shah Palace, Don Bosco, Jama Masjid, and more. I just followed the glowing hexes again. Homes at risk went 29 -> 13. At 2049 the camera moved to Campal, where there was a big block of red-striped homes.
- **2050, last build.** Maya: "Last call! 12 homes are still in danger, most of them around Campal." My biodiversity was +56, so I thought I could afford the seawall's -5. I put it on the beach right in front of the red Campal homes.
- **Cyclone + flood 2050.** I watched this one without speeding up. It looked cool: a storm swirl, rain, and the blocks going dark red. **12 homes lost, the exact Campal ones behind my seawall.** 2 stars, because 3 stars allow 8 lost at most. "What protected" said **Seawall 0** again.
- **Final card.** 105/123 homes saved, biodiversity +51, **8 of 9 stars, Panjim cleared.**

## 3. What confused me

- **Seawall, "the strongest wall", saved 0 homes, twice.** In 2050 I put it right in front of the 12 homes Maya named, and all 12 were lost. The menu never says it only works against some kinds of water, or that it has to face the sea. *What would have helped:* when I pick Seawall on a tile where it won't help, show "won't protect anyone here", or give a preview like "homes at risk 12 -> 12" before I confirm.
- **Where was the water coming from at Campal?** It looked like it came from the river side, not the beach, but I only worked that out from the storm animation. *What would help:* show arrows for where the water comes in on the at-risk homes before the storm.
- **"Build 1 khazan in Merces".** I didn't know where Merces was. Zooming out found it, but a "take me there" button or an arrow on the task card would help.
- **"Build 2 khazans in Merces 0/2".** This showed 0/2 even though I'd already built one in Merces in 2029. Did that one count or not?
- **"7 khazans at 5 places, shown on the map".** I could only see 2 to 4 outlined hexes at a time. I didn't know where the other places were until the camera moved.
- **Maya vs the task card.** Maya said "strengthen Taleigao now" while the task said "mangrove near Atal Setu". Which one should I do?
- **"Miramar Beach: Damaged" and "Goa State Museum: Damaged"** were shown even on 3-star results. Is damaged bad? Does it cost anything?
- **The camera moved by itself** a few times (2040 and 2049). My clicks on a hex sometimes landed somewhere else than I expected after that.
- **The star rules** are only explained on the very last card, in a long game rule. I would have liked "3 stars = lose at most 8" before the final storm, so I knew what to aim for.

## 4. Where I was bored

- **The middle of each prep block.** Click glowing hex, click the "Objective" option, repeat, about 8 times. There's no real decision, the game basically tells me where to put things.
- **Maya's fact bubbles**, like "Research in England and Wales found...". I didn't read them.
- **Replaying 2026-2030 after the kick-out.** Same 5 builds again, pure boredom.

## 5. Where I smiled

- "Homes at risk" dropping 9 -> 3 -> 0 with each build. Instant reward.
- Getting 3 stars on the first cyclone in run 2 after learning that the seawall was a waste.
- The bird flying over the map.
- The final storm swirl and rain. It looks dramatic.
- "Panjim cleared" with 8 of 9 stars.
- Seeing real places I know: Miramar, Campal, Dona Paula, the Don Bosco school.

## 6. Bugs and glitches

1. **MAJOR (possibly blocker): the game jumps back to the main menu mid-run and all progress is lost.**
   - Steps: Play Panjim -> Begin -> 5 builds -> cyclone -> result cards -> On to 2031 -> plant a mangrove in Merces (2032) -> click a second glowing hex in Merces -> pick Mangrove.
   - What I got: the main menu, with no "Still there?" warning that I saw. Play Panjim restarted at 2026, year 1, with every build gone. Right after that, it **dropped me to the menu again** about 30 to 60 seconds after I pressed Play Panjim, while the intro card was showing and before I clicked Begin.
   - It didn't happen again in run 2 at the same click, so it isn't tied to that click. My real gaps between clicks were sometimes 30 to 90 seconds while I looked at the screen, but never 3 minutes.
   - Expected: stay in the game, or at least warn me first and let me resume.
   - Screenshot: student-kicked-to-menu.jpg. The console showed nothing.
2. **Major (design or bug): the seawall protects 0 homes even when placed right in front of the homes at risk** (2030, and 2050 at Campal), and the game gives no hint before I confirm. Screenshots: student-seawall-saved-0.jpg, student-final-storm-campal-lost.jpg. Expected: either it helps a bit, or the game warns me it won't.
3. **Minor: map labels overlap.** "Taleigao" draws on top of "The Mandovi waterfront", so it reads "Taleigao Jovi waterfront" (2045 to 2049). In 2041 there are about 12 landmark labels piled up over Campal and Altinho. Screenshot: student-label-overlap-campal-risk.jpg.
4. **Minor: the Maya "Got it" bubble at the start vanished before I could click it**, and clicking "Got it" said there was no such button. It wasn't a problem, just odd.
5. **Minor: the khazan I built in Merces in 2029 (a "get ahead" task) didn't seem to count** toward the 2031 "Build 2 khazans in Merces 0/2".
6. **Minor: the camera re-centres by itself between builds** (2040, 2049), so a hex I was aiming at moves away.
7. **Minor: just after the first cyclone, a screenshot still showed the "Replay" panel** while the screen text already had the result card. It caught up after a second.
8. **Console:** empty both times I checked (mid-run and at the end).

## 7. Fun signals

- **First build:** about 28 seconds, 4 actions (Play Panjim, Begin, hex, Pandanus).
- **Whole run:** run 2 alone was about 6 to 7 minutes of real-player time. Including the lost run 1 and the restart, about 9 minutes.
- **My real choices** (at least two good options with different outcomes): about 4.
  1. 2030, last build before the cyclone: seawall vs dune. A seawall gave 2 stars, a dune gave 3. That's a real lesson.
  2. 2039: follow Maya ("strengthen Taleigao") or the task (Atal Setu mangrove). I did both, one each.
  3. 2050, last build: seawall vs dune/pandanus at Campal. I picked the seawall and it did nothing. Maybe a dune would have done nothing too, I don't know.
  4. "Try this storm again" vs keep going after the 2-star cyclone in run 1.

  Most other builds weren't choices. I just clicked the glowing hex and the "Objective" option.
- **Did one pattern always win?** Yes. Following the glowing "Objective" hexes was enough for 3, 3 and 2 stars and a clear. Khazans plus mangroves did nearly all the flood saving (khazan 31 and 34, mangrove 18 and 24). The seawall saved 0 every time.
- **Storm results that surprised me:**
  - Seawall 0 in 2030.
  - 12 homes lost in 2050 right behind my brand-new seawall.
  - The 2050 storm had "57 homes in its path", exactly the same number as the 2040 flood.

## 9. Would I play again? Would I tell a friend?

- **Play again: maybe yes.** I want that ninth star and to find out what actually saves Campal. But if it kicks me to the menu again and wipes my game, then no.
- **Tell a friend: yes, kind of.** I'd say: "It's a Goa storm game on a real map of Panjim. It's easy: just follow the glowing hexes. Don't build seawalls, they do nothing. And don't leave it alone, it might reset on you."

## 10. Verdict

Fun, fast, and pretty, with a satisfying "homes at risk" countdown, but it mostly plays itself through glowing hexes, the seawall is a confusing trap, and a reset back to the menu wiped my first run.

## Knowledge questions (asked after play)

1. **What does a mangrove do in a storm?** The build menu said it slows cyclone surge and waves, and river floods too. Maya also said the mangroves on a khazan's outer bund work as wave breakers against the tide. In my 2040 flood it saved 18 homes, and 24 in 2050.
2. **Why can a seawall be risky?** The menu said it "costs nature, and cracks with age". Maya said it holds back a surge only until it breaks, in its second cyclone or its next one once it's 10 years old. For me it also cost me a star by lowering biodiversity, and it saved 0 homes both times.
3. **What is a khazan, and what is it for?** Maya said khazans are Goa's coastal wetlands, reclaimed from marsh, with bunds that keep out the tide, and farmers and fishers share the land. In the game it holds back river floods: "Khazans held the floodwater in their fields, so the homes nearby stayed drier."
4. **Where did the 2040 flood come from, and why from there rather than the sea?** Maya said monsoon rain swells the Mandovi upstream, and the flood comes down the river through the channel at Ribandar into Merces, St Cruz and Taleigao. So it came from the river because it was rain water, not a sea surge. The game didn't say any more than that about why not the sea.
5. **What helps homes against a cyclone's wind?** Pandanus: the menu said it "shields homes from cyclone waves and wind". It was the only build that mentioned wind.
